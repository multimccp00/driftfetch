Your downloads are your business.

No account, no ads, no tracking, not even on this website. Here’s exactly what stays on your PC and what leaves it, in plain words.

Stays on your PC

Your settings and history

Everything you’ve downloaded and how you like things set up.

Site sign-ins

Encrypted with your Windows account, so only you can read them on this PC.

All in one folder

Settings › Advanced shows where it is. Delete it and DriftFetch forgets everything.

Leaves your PC

Requests to the sites you download from

The same requests your browser would make when you watch the video.

A check with GitHub for engine updates

Updates are verified against the maintainers’ signatures before they install. You can turn this off.

Nothing to us

There’s no DriftFetch server and no analytics. We couldn’t see your downloads if we wanted to.

This website

No cookies

So there’s no cookie banner to click away.

No analytics

No visitor counting, no fingerprinting, no third-party scripts.

Self-hosted fonts

Fonts load from this site, not from a font service.
