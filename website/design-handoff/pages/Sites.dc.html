Works with the sites you already use.

DriftFetch is built on yt-dlp and gallery-dl, two open-source projects that between them understand well over a thousand sites. Here are the ones people use most.

Most popular

YouTubeVideos, playlists and channels in the best quality offered. RedditVideos with their sound, and image galleries in order. VimeoPublic and unlisted videos, in the quality the creator allows.

Full lists: yt-dlp supported sites and gallery-dl supported sites. Support depends on each site and can change when a site updates.

Good to know

Some sites need a sign-in

Members-only or age-restricted content asks you to sign in once inside the app. The sign-in stays encrypted on your PC.

No DRM, no paywalls

DriftFetch doesn’t remove copy protection or get around payment. Streaming services like Netflix won’t work.

A site stopped working?

Sites change. Engine updates usually fix it within days. If not, report it on GitHub.
