Accessibility

We aim for this website and the app to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Both work fully with a keyboard, show a visible focus outline, keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher, and respect your system’s “reduce motion” setting. The decorative line that follows your scrolling is hidden from screen readers and stops moving when motion is reduced.
