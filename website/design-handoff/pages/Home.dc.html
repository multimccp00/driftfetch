Download for Windows Free and open source · Windows 10 and 11 · Version 0.2.1

Three steps. You only do the first one.

  1. 01

    Copy a link

    From your browser, a chat or an email. Anywhere Windows lets you copy. That’s your part done.

  2. 02

    DriftFetch finds it

    It checks what the link points to and picks the best quality the site offers. Albums and playlists wait for you to choose.

  3. 03

    It’s on your PC

    Saved to Downloads\\DriftFetch, sorted by site. Open the file or its folder straight from the list.

It tells you what’s actually going on.

A lot of downloaders turn everything red the moment a site says “slow down”. DriftFetch gives each situation its own name, colour and next step, so you only act when you really have to.

And when you want the details, they’re all there.

Every download in one list, with filters, a details panel and settings in plain words. This is the real interface, so click around.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Not even on this website.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, your download history and any site sign-ins. Sign-ins are encrypted with your Windows account. All of it lives in one folder you can delete at any time.

What we see

Nothing. There is no DriftFetch server. The app only talks to the sites you download from, and to GitHub when it checks for engine updates.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts. So there’s no cookie banner to click away. Read the privacy policy.

Built on open tools that many people already trust: yt-dlp, gallery-dl, FFmpeg and Deno. Engine updates are checked against the maintainers’ signatures before they install.
